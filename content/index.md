This is Andy's personal wiki — a living knowledge base for homelab configurations, self-hosted services, and the occasional hard-won troubleshooting victory.

## What's here

- **[[homelab/|Homelab]]** — Docker stacks, Home Assistant, AdGuard, and everything running on the NAS
- **[[jellyfin/|Jellyfin]]** — Media server setup, GPU transcoding, and hardware details

## Why this exists

Every homelabber has that moment — you fix something at 2 AM, forget to document it, and six months later you're solving the same problem from scratch. This wiki is the antidote. If it took more than five minutes to figure out, it belongs here.

Built with [Quartz 4](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz) and the [80s-neon](https://github.com/saberzero1/quartz-themes/tree/master/themes/80s-neon) theme. Hosted on a UGREEN NAS because why not.
